# Podcast Reels — dynamic captions

Source: `podcast_reels_9x16_draft.mp4`.

1080 × 1920, 30 fps. Bold Rubik captions appear word by word, with a yellow current word, white previous words, dark outline, and a short pop animation. Captions stay in the lower part of the image and clear during longer pauses.

The Russian transcript was cross-checked with two speech recognizers. Word times use acoustic forced alignment, with a separate speech activity and waveform check of pauses. The source starts during a question, so its first caption retains the audible fragment with an ellipsis. The attached SRT contains phrase timing; animated word timing is burned into the MP4.

The original AAC audio is copied without re-encoding. The export is checked for all 1,052 source video frames, unchanged audio duration and decoded audio hash, monotonic caption timing, and full error-free decoding. A frame sample covers the beginning, middle, end, and long captions.
